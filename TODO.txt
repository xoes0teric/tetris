Tetris game rules:

1. Board
10 collumns wide 20 rows tall

2. Shapes
I piece 4 blocks long (light blue)
O piece 2x2 (yellow)
T piece 3 blocks with 1 sticking out (purple)
S piece 2x2 but offset to the right (green)
Z piece 2x2 but offset to the left (red)
L piece L shape (orange)
J piece L shape pointing left (dark blue)

3. Movement
Players can:
move falling pieces left or right
rotate them 90 degrees clockwise or counterclockwise
speed up their descent or instantly drop to the bottom

4. Scoring
Fill every square in a horizontal row without any gaps to clear the line
Clearing four lines at the same time awards the highest points
Levels: As you clear more lines, the game level increases, causing the pieces to fall faster

5. Losing
The game ends when new pieces can no longer enter the playfield because the existing stack has reached the top of the screen
